# FloodGuard AI 🌊🛡️

**FloodGuard AI** is an intelligent flood monitoring, early-warning, and risk assessment platform powered by AI.

## Overview
- **Real-Time Monitoring & Alerts**: Live water level tracking and predictive early flood alerts.
- **AI Risk Assessment**: Machine learning models assessing precipitation, terrain vulnerability, and water flow dynamics.
- **Interactive Geospatial Dashboard**: Map visualizer highlighting flood zones, evacuation routes, and emergency shelter locations.
- **Disaster Response & Evacuation Assistant**: AI-driven emergency routing and community emergency guidance.
